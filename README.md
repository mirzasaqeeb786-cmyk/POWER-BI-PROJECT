# ☕ Cafe Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
The Cafe Sales Analysis Dashboard is a data visualization project designed to analyze cafe sales performance. It provides insights into total sales, transactions, quantity sold, average order value, item-wise sales, payment methods, and monthly sales trends.

The dashboard helps understand customer purchasing behavior and identify the best-performing products.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze the overall sales performance of the cafe.
- Identify the highest and lowest-selling items.
- Analyze transactions based on payment methods.
- Understand monthly sales trends.
- Monitor important business performance indicators (KPIs).
- Support better business decision-making through data visualization.

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard displays four important KPIs:

- **Total Sales:** 47.30K
- **Total Transactions:** 5K
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 16K
- **Average Order Value:** 8.98

### 2. Total Sales by Item
A bar chart represents sales performance across different cafe products.

Items included:
- Salad
- Smoothie
- Sandwich
- Juice
- Cake
- Coffee
- Tea
- Cookie

### 3. Transactions by Payment Method
A pie chart displays the distribution of transactions by payment method:
- Digital Wallet
- Credit Card
- Cash
- Unknown

### 4. Monthly Sales Analysis
A line chart represents monthly sales trends from January to December. It helps identify changes in sales performance throughout the year.

### 5. Interactive Filter
The dashboard includes an Item slicer that allows users to filter sales data based on individual products.

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Salad has the highest sales among the displayed items.
- Cookie has the lowest sales.
- Digital Wallet, Credit Card, and Cash contribute almost equally to payment transactions.
- Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the year.
- The dashboard provides a quick overview of overall cafe performance.
